### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a software developer and Master of Science student in Computer Science at **Université Laval** in Québec City, Canada. 

My work combines **full-stack product development** with **applied artificial intelligence** and **cybersecurity research**. At **[SYSMOTIX](https://sysmotix.com/en)**, I design and build production web applications, operational workflows, and private enterprise software products.

- 🎓 **M.Sc. in Computer Science**: Graduate research centered on applied AI and cybersecurity.
- ⚙️ **Product Engineering**: Developing end-to-end web and cross-platform applications.
- 🛡️ **Security-Oriented**: Dedicated to reproducible experiments, strict automated testing, and resilient architectures.
